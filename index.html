ABOUT / 04

“Can this actually work?” is where I like to start.

I’m a developer drawn to the moment an idea becomes a working product. That means thinking past the mockup: what powers it, how information moves, how money is recorded, and how it can grow without becoming fragile.

My work sits across frontend, backend, mobile, databases, and deployment—especially with Python/FastAPI and Flutter/Dart. I value clear structure, practical trade-offs, and software that respects the people using it.
